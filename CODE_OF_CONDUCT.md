# Code of Conduct

**TL;DR — don't be a dick.**

**In short: be constructive and respectful.**

## Our standard

If you need the longer version: participation in this repository should
remain welcoming, constructive and focused on improving the software. This
applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed spaces.

- Be kind to other contributors. Constructive criticism is welcome;
  personal attacks, harassment, and pile-ons are not.
- Assume good faith. Most rough edges in PR review are translation
  problems between people with different contexts, not malice.
- Don't share anyone's private information (contact details,
  location, real-name unmaskings) without their explicit consent.
- Respect a request to stop unwanted contact or move an argument back to the
  technical issue.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer at
`iohann.s.titov@gmail.com`, including links or screenshots and any context
needed to understand the incident. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical. This small project has no independent moderation body. If a
report concerns the maintainer, that limitation should be understood up front;
conduct on GitHub that violates the platform's policies may also be reported
through [GitHub's safety and abuse-reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github).

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, reject
contributions, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. The response will be proportionate to the conduct and its
effect on other participants.
